
# Research Synthesizer AI

An AI-powered multi-agent research assistant designed to analyze academic papers, compare findings, identify research gaps, and generate structured research reports.

## Project Overview

Research Synthesizer AI aims to simplify the process of reviewing multiple research documents by coordinating three specialized AI agents.

## Planned AI Agents

### 1. Researcher Agent
- Extract text from PDF documents.
- Split long documents into smaller chunks.
- Summarize key research findings.

### 2. Critic Agent
- Compare findings across multiple papers.
- Identify contradictions and research gaps.
- Generate critical questions about the evidence.

### 3. Writer Agent
- Combine research summaries and critical insights.
- Generate structured executive summaries.
- Prepare reports for Markdown and PDF export.
